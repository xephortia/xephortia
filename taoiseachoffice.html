Taoiseach's Office

The Taoiseach's Office is the Office of the Executive Head of State and is held by the sitting Taoiseach and potential staff.

Its goal is to exercise the Executive branch's decisions, manage internal affairs. It ratifies all legislative acts and international treaties passed by the National Council, forms the National Council, appoints Ministers, and manages all internal affairs like Visas and Citizenship, and oversees the National Security Forces' Policing, similar to how a Ministry of Interior would operate.
