Government Support

Need assistance from the Xephortian Government? You can contact Government Support by these methods during the hours listed below.

Government Support Email

Email: xephortia@gmail.com.

Emails are available 24 hours a day from Monday to Thursday, however please note the service is operated from the British/Irish Standard Time zones (named Xephortian Standard Time).

Exceptions

Weekends: Friday to Sunday

Your email may not be answered during days listed. If you need to contact in an emergency during these days, please contact via email or go to Xephortia's discord server for further assistance.
